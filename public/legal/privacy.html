3. Why we process it, and our legal basis

  • Providing accounts and saved history — performance of our contract with you (Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Processing subscriptions and payments — performance of the contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) and compliance with accounting/tax obligations (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Security, fraud prevention and service reliability — our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)).

4. Processors and third parties

We share data only with service providers acting on our behalf under data-processing agreements:

  • Supabase — authentication and database for your account, saved zones, equipment library, operational-volume plans and pilot flight records (hosted within the European Union).
  • Render — hosting of the application and its server logs.
  • Resend — delivery of transactional emails (such as your passwordless "magic link"); it processes your email address to send them.
  • Stripe — payment processing and subscription management. Stripe is the processor of your payment data as an independent controller for its own compliance purposes; see stripe.com/privacy.
  • Oblio — issuing the fiscal invoices required by Romanian law (including e-Factura). It receives the billing details above — your name and address, and, where provided, your CNP, or your company name and CIF/CUI — to generate the invoices.
  • OpenStreetMap — map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap servers, which necessarily receive your IP address to serve the imagery.

5. International transfers

Some processors (e.g. Stripe) may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers are protected by adequacy decisions or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

Account and saved-zone data are kept for as long as your account is active. You may delete saved zones at any time, and you can delete your account together with all associated data yourself in the app (👤 Profile → Privacy). Deleting your account also cancels any active subscription. Invoices already issued are retained by our invoicing provider (Oblio) for the period required by Romanian fiscal law (10 years). Server logs are kept for a limited period for security purposes.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and to object to processing, as well as to withdraw consent where processing is based on it.

You can exercise access, portability and erasure yourself at any time in the app under 👤 Profile → Privacy: download all of your data as a JSON file, or permanently delete your account. These options remain available even if you have no active subscription. For any other request, contact us at office@zorile-energie.ro.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Romanian supervisory authority, the Autoritatea Națională de Supraveghere a Prelucrării Datelor cu Caracter Personal (ANSPDCP) — dataprotection.ro.

8. Cookies and local storage

The Service uses only strictly necessary cookies and browser storage. See our Cookie Policy for details.
